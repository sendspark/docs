## Sending dynamic videos with PlusVibe

The Sendspark + PlusVibe integration lets you send dynamic videos in your PlusVibe cold email campaigns. Instead of recording a separate video for each prospect, create one video and Sendspark automatically personalizes it for every recipient — greeting them by name, showing their website, and more.

PlusVibe (formerly pipl.ai) is an AI-powered cold email automation platform built for B2B sales teams, founders, and lead generation agencies. It combines email sequencing, warm-up, deliverability management, and AI-driven personalization into one platform.

## How to send dynamic videos in PlusVibe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish your dynamic video campaign">
    In Sendspark, create and generate your Dynamic Video campaign. If you're new to dynamic videos, check out our [getting started guide](/create-a-dynamic-video-campaign) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the PlusVibe snippet">
    In Sendspark, click **Share Campaign** and select **PlusVibe** from the list of compatible platforms. This copies the share snippet to your clipboard. The snippet contains an animated thumbnail and a "Watch video" link with the `{{email}}` merge tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the snippet into a PlusVibe email step">
    In PlusVibe, create or edit an email step in your campaign sequence. Paste the Sendspark snippet directly into the email body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your email">
    In PlusVibe's editor, you'll see the default version of your video (a video of yourself). This is expected — the video personalizes automatically once the email is sent to each contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to the right contacts">
    Make sure the contacts in your PlusVibe campaign match the prospects you generated videos for in Sendspark. When PlusVibe sends the email, each recipient's `{{email}}` is replaced with their actual email address. Sendspark uses this to match the contact to their personalized video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To be safe, click **Download CSV** in Sendspark to get a clean list of contacts with successfully generated videos. Upload that list to PlusVibe so you don't email anyone who didn't get a video.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What format does the PlusVibe snippet use?">
    The PlusVibe snippet uses the same format as Apollo: an animated thumbnail GIF linked to the personalized video page, plus a "Watch video" text link. Both links contain the `{{email}}` variable so Sendspark can match each recipient to their video.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does PlusVibe's email warm-up help with dynamic video emails?">
    Yes. PlusVibe's built-in warm-up and deliverability tools help ensure your dynamic video emails reach the inbox instead of spam. This is especially useful when sending video emails at scale.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track video views when sending through PlusVibe?">
    Yes. All video views, play events, and engagement data appear in your Sendspark dashboard regardless of which platform you use to send the email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why should I download Sendspark's CSV instead of using my own list?">
    When you generate videos for many contacts, some may not render — for example, a prospect's website may fail to load. Sendspark's exported CSV only includes contacts whose video was successfully generated, so you don't email anyone who would see a broken placeholder.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I know PlusVibe as pipl.ai — is this the same platform?">
    Yes. PlusVibe was formerly known as pipl.ai. The Sendspark integration works the same way regardless of which name you see.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
