Sendspark works with any email platform that lets you edit HTML, because that's what the snippet code needs to render your thumbnail, AI voice cloning, text variables, and dynamic background. Find your platform in the lists below to jump straight to its guide, or check the live list at [sendspark.com/integrations](https://www.sendspark.com/integrations).

## Key takeaways

- Pick your platform from the lists below. Each links to a step-by-step guide.
- **Dynamic videos in bulk** means one snippet personalizes a different video for every lead on your list.
- **Personalized videos at scale** means you pair pre-recorded videos with your contacts.
- **Quick share** means pasting a thumbnail into a single email from your own inbox or marketing tool.
- Your platform needs **HTML editing** for the snippet to render correctly.
- No guide for your platform? The snippet works anywhere HTML editing does, and you can [request a platform](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSerf7BF0uD1jM9DjF_GSAefGNJtwzG1gRFCV8yoGDQMb-DACQ/viewform?hsCtaTracking=87ed9c2b-b05f-4cf9-9789-0319fe75988d%7C993d2c23-8a2b-44ed-8e7f-6573cadea53c).

## How to embed a Sendspark thumbnail in an email

The steps are the same on every platform:

1. **Create your video or campaign** in Sendspark.
2. **Copy the snippet code** once the video is ready.
3. **Paste the snippet** into the HTML editing section of your email. ![HTML editing section in email automation platform with Sendspark video snippet code pasted](/images/9583536/edb9250f-image.png)

Recipients who click the thumbnail land on your video, so you collect view and engagement analytics. If the thumbnail renders as broken or as plain text, your platform is most likely stripping the HTML, which is the one requirement the snippet can't work around.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see which platforms are supported?">
    The lists on this page cover every platform with a written guide. For the live product list, see [sendspark.com/integrations](https://www.sendspark.com/integrations), or [Settings → Integrations](https://sendspark.com/settings/integrations) if you're already a member.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My platform isn't listed. Can I still send Sendspark videos?">
    Usually yes. The snippet works in any platform that supports HTML editing, so a missing guide means nobody has written one rather than that the platform won't work. You can also [request a platform](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSerf7BF0uD1jM9DjF_GSAefGNJtwzG1gRFCV8yoGDQMb-DACQ/viewform?hsCtaTracking=87ed9c2b-b05f-4cf9-9789-0319fe75988d%7C993d2c23-8a2b-44ed-8e7f-6573cadea53c) and the team will review it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I don't use a CRM or email tool at all?">
    You can still send dynamic videos in bulk. See [sharing dynamic videos without sales email tools](/share-dynamic-videos-without-sales-email-tools), which covers getting the videos out using the campaign's own export rather than an integration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Sendspark send the emails itself?">
    No. Sendspark creates and personalizes the videos, and you send them from your own email tool, which is why HTML editing matters. See [can you send emails from Sendspark](/can-you-send-emails-from-sendspark).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="HubSpot is warning me that my module can contain HubL code. Is that a problem?">
    No, it's a false positive from HubSpot's default behaviour rather than anything wrong with the Sendspark snippet. See [removing HubSpot's HubL code warning](/how-to-remove-hubspot-s-hubl-code-warning-message).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between dynamic and personalized videos?">
    A dynamic video campaign generates a separate video for each lead, so one snippet personalizes across your whole list. Personalized videos at scale pair videos you recorded in advance with your contacts. Dynamic suits cold outreach to a large list; personalized suits a smaller set you want to speak to directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my platform need to support HTML editing?">
    HTML editing lets you paste Sendspark's snippet code into your email. That snippet is what powers AI voice cloning, text variables, and the correct dynamic background, so the video thumbnail displays properly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I embed a Sendspark thumbnail in an email?">
    Create your video or campaign, copy the provided snippet code, then paste it into the HTML editing section of your email. When recipients click the thumbnail, they're directed to your video and you collect viewer analytics.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
