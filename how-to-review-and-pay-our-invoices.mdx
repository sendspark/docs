To review your invoices, open your account **Menu**, click **BILLING**, then scroll down and click the arrow on the right to expand your billing history. From there you can view an invoice online or download it. To pay manually, click **View Invoice** and then **Pay Invoice Online**.

## Key takeaways

- Invoices live under your account **Menu → BILLING**. Scroll to the bottom and click the arrow on the right to expand your billing history.
- Each invoice can be viewed online or downloaded.
- To pay an invoice manually, click **View Invoice**, then head to **Pay Invoice Online**.

In this article, you will learn how to access your invoices

1) Head to your account Menu and click on BILLING:

![The Sendspark account menu, with Billing among its entries](/images/8856714/ba5b04ca-image.png)

2) Scroll all the way down and click on the arrow to the right to deploy the invoices:

![Billing History](/images/8856645/cb0e9746-image.png)

3) You can then View the Invoice online or download the invoice:

![Invoices](/images/8856645/13827907-image.png)

4) If you need to pay an Invoice manually, click on VIEW INVOICE and then head to Pay Invoice Online.

![Pay Invoice Online](/images/8856645/20e6904a-image.png)

If the payment doesn't go through, the card on file may be out of date. [Update your payment method](/how-to-update-your-payment-method), then pay the invoice again. If the card is current but still gets declined, [your card may not support this payment](/why-your-card-doesn-t-support-this-payment), which is common for cards issued in India.
