Team Insights is your workspace-level analytics dashboard. Instead of opening one video at a time, you see how everyone's videos performed together — who sent the most videos, whose videos get watched, and which content earns the most clicks. Open it from **Team Insights** in the left sidebar.

## Key takeaways

- Open **Team Insights** from the left sidebar. It's the workspace-wide view, separate from the per-video Analytics modal.
- Pick a period with the range control: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** (the default), or a **custom range** going back up to 180 days.
- **Top performers** crowns a #1 for four measures: most views, best conversion, most active, and most engaging.
- The **Member leaderboard** lists your team with Videos, Views, CTAs, and Likes — click any column heading to rank by it.
- Everything is attributed to the **member who created the campaign**, so a member's numbers follow the videos they made.
- Build the dashboard you want: add and rearrange widgets, save layouts as named **reports**, and **export to PDF**.

## Open Team Insights

Click **Team Insights** in the left sidebar. The dashboard loads with your saved layout and your last-used reporting period.

<Note>
  Team Insights replaces the old **Team Overview** entry in the sidebar. Everything Team Overview showed is available here, alongside the new widgets.
</Note>

## Choose your reporting period

The range control in the header sets the window for **every** widget on the dashboard at once. You can pick:

- **Last 7 days**
- **Last 30 days**
- **Last 90 days** — the default
- **Custom range** — any start and end date, going back up to 180 days from today

If we hold less data than the range you asked for, the header names the range actually covered and notes that it was **shortened from** the range you picked. That's a data-availability limit, not an error: the numbers on screen are correct for the shorter window shown.

## Focus the dashboard with filters

Two filters sit next to the range control, and both narrow every widget at once:

- **Member** — shows only the campaigns that member created
- **Campaign** — shows only that campaign

You can apply one, both, or clear them independently. Filters are temporary: they reset when you leave the dashboard and are not stored inside a saved report.

## Top performers

Four cards, each naming the **#1** member for one measure over the selected period:

| Card | What it ranks | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Most views** | Total views their videos earned | Number of views |
| **Best conversion** | Share of viewers who clicked a call-to-action | % CTA rate |
| **Most active** | Number of videos they created | Videos sent |
| **Most engaging** | Average share of each video watched | % average watch |

Each card is ranked on its own, so one member can lead every card or none of them. A card stays blank when nobody qualifies for that measure yet — for example, nobody can hold a CTA rate until their videos have been viewed. When the whole period has no activity, the widget shows a single empty state instead of four blank cards.

## Member leaderboard

The leaderboard lists your team members with four columns:

- **Videos** — videos the member created in the period
- **Views** — how many times their video pages were opened
- **CTAs** — call-to-action clicks their videos earned
- **Likes** — thumbs-up likes currently in place on their videos

Click any column heading to rank by it. Ranking is always highest-first, the top three get 🥇🥈🥉, and your own row is tagged **you**. Under each name you'll see their job title, or **Workspace owner** for the owner.

Teams of ten or fewer are shown in full, including members sitting at zero. In a larger workspace the board shows the top ten members who have a number in the column you sorted by, and the footnote tells you how many members were left out — sort by a different column to surface whoever leads it.

<Note>
  The footnote **"Views count per video: one visit on two videos counts twice"** explains why the leaderboard's views can add up to more than the workspace total elsewhere on the dashboard. The leaderboard counts views per video, so one person who opens two of your videos is counted on both.
</Note>

## How each metric is counted

Team Insights is deliberately strict about what counts, so the numbers hold up when you share them:

- **Attribution follows the campaign creator.** A member's figures cover the campaigns they created, and never change hands afterwards.
- **Videos** counts what the member created, whether or not anyone watched. A member whose videos went unwatched still shows the videos they made.
- **Views** counts video page opens, the same way the per-video [Analytics modal](/how-to-understand-video-analytics) does — opening a page is not the same as playing the video.
- **Likes** counts likes that are still in place. If a viewer likes a video and then removes the like, it doesn't count, and liking twice doesn't count twice.
- **CTA rate** counts people, not clicks: the share of unique viewers who clicked a call-to-action. One viewer clicking twice is one conversion.
- **Average watch** is weighted across every video and visit in the period, so one fully-watched video can't outweigh hundreds that were watched most of the way through.
- **Rates are left blank, never zero,** when there's nothing to divide by. A member with no views yet shows an empty CTA rate rather than 0%.

## Customize your dashboard

- **Add widget** opens a drawer of everything available, grouped by category, with the recommended set called out. Your dashboard starts with KPI cards, Activity over time, Top performers, Member leaderboard, and Top videos.
- **Drag** a widget by its header to move it, and drag its corner to resize. The grid reflows around it.
- **Reset layout** puts the recommended set back.

Some widgets are available on higher plans. Those appear in the drawer marked as locked so you can see what's included before upgrading.

## Save dashboards as reports

A report is a named dashboard layout, so you can keep one view for your weekly team review and another for a single campaign.

Open the reports menu in the header to:

- **New report** — save the current layout under a name
- **Duplicate** — copy the report you're on as a starting point
- **Rename** — rename the current report
- **Set as default** — make it the report that opens first
- **Delete** — remove a report you no longer need

Every workspace starts with the **Default** report, which can be reset but not deleted. Reports are yours: saving one doesn't change what your teammates see.

## Export to PDF

Click **Export PDF** to save the dashboard as it appears — the widgets, the reporting period, and any filters you applied are all named in the export, so the file still makes sense weeks later. Wait until the widgets have finished loading before exporting, so nothing lands in the file half-drawn.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does a member show videos but no views?">
    Videos counts what they created; views counts what recipients opened. A member who created videos that nobody has opened yet will show videos with zero views. It's the most common pattern for videos sent in the last day or two.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a member's CTA rate blank instead of 0%?">
    A rate needs viewers to divide by. Until someone has viewed that member's videos there's no denominator, so we leave the rate blank rather than credit them with a 0% they never earned. The same applies to average watch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do removed likes still count?">
    No. The Likes column counts likes that are currently in place. If a viewer likes a video and then unlikes it, the like disappears from the count — and liking, unliking, then liking again counts once, not three times.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the leaderboard's views add up to more than the workspace total?">
    The leaderboard counts views per video. One person who opens two of your team's videos in the same visit is counted once on each video, so per-member views can sum to more than the workspace's count of distinct visits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the header say my range was shortened?">
    You asked for a window that reaches further back than the data we hold. The dashboard shows the range it could actually cover and says what you originally asked for, so you're never comparing a 90-day figure against a shorter one by accident.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My team has more than ten people — where is everyone?">
    The board shows the top ten for whichever column you sorted by, and the footnote says how many members aren't shown. Sort by a different column to see who leads that one. Everyone is included in the ranking; only the display is trimmed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does filtering by member change the leaderboard?">
    Yes — every widget on the dashboard, including the leaderboard and Top performers, follows the filters and the reporting period you've set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see my saved reports?">
    Reports are per user. Creating, renaming, or deleting one only affects your own dashboard, so you can experiment without disrupting your team's view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from a single video's analytics?">
    The per-video <a href="/how-to-understand-video-analytics">Analytics modal</a> answers "who watched this video". Team Insights answers "how is the team doing" across every campaign in the period, broken down by the member who created it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
