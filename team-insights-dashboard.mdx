Team Insights is your workspace-level analytics dashboard. Instead of opening one video at a time, you see how everyone's videos performed together: who sent the most videos, whose videos get watched, and which content earns the most clicks. Open it from **Team Insights** in the left sidebar.

<Note>
  **Team Insights is in beta.** The dashboard is live and its numbers are real, but we're still adding widgets and refining the experience. You'll see a **Beta** label next to it in the sidebar. The **Add widget** drawer also lists widgets we're still building, which show a coming soon placeholder once added. See [what's available today](#which-widgets-are-available) below.
</Note>

## Key takeaways

- Open **Team Insights** from the left sidebar. It's the workspace-wide view, separate from the per-video Analytics modal.
- Pick a period with the range control: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** (the default), or a **custom range** going back up to 180 days.
- **Top performers** crowns a #1 for four measures: most views, best conversion, most active, and most engaging.
- The **Member leaderboard** lists your team with Videos, Views, CTAs, and Likes. Click any column heading to rank by it.
- Everything is attributed to the **member who created the campaign**, so a member's numbers follow the videos they made.
- Build the dashboard you want: add and rearrange widgets, save layouts as named **reports**, and **export to PDF**.
- Every plan can open Team Insights. **Solo** shows the recommended widget set, and **Growth** and above unlock every widget.
- Figures are cached, so allow two to three minutes for a new video or a fresh view to appear.

## Open Team Insights

Click **Team Insights** in the left sidebar. The dashboard loads with your saved layout and your last-used reporting period.

<Note>
  While Team Insights is in beta it **takes the place of Team Overview** in the sidebar, so you'll find your team's numbers here instead. Most figures Team Overview showed have a widget on this dashboard, which you can add from **Add widget**. One difference to plan around: Team Overview reported all-time totals, while Team Insights reports on a period reaching back at most 180 days, so all-time figures aren't available here yet.
</Note>

## Choose your reporting period

The range control in the header sets the window for **every** widget on the dashboard at once. You can pick:

- **Last 7 days**
- **Last 30 days**
- **Last 90 days**, the default
- **Custom range**, any start and end date, going back up to 180 days from today

If we hold less data than the range you asked for, the header names the range actually covered and notes that it was **shortened from** the range you picked. That's a data-availability limit, not an error: the numbers on screen are correct for the shorter window shown.

## Focus the dashboard with filters

Two dropdowns sit in the toolbar, and both narrow every widget at once:

- **All members**: pick a member to see only the campaigns they created
- **All campaigns**: pick a campaign to see only that one

You can apply one, both, or clear them independently. Filters are temporary: they reset when you leave the dashboard and are not stored inside a saved report.

## Top performers

Four cards, each naming the **#1** member for one measure over the selected period:

| Card | What it ranks | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Most views** | Total views their videos earned | Number of views |
| **Best conversion** | Share of viewers who clicked a call-to-action | % CTA rate |
| **Most active** | Number of videos they created | Videos sent |
| **Most engaging** | Average share of each video watched | % average watch |

Each card is ranked on its own, so one member can lead every card or none of them. A card stays blank only while nobody qualifies for that measure yet. Nobody can hold a CTA rate, for example, until their videos have been viewed.

Once there are views to divide by, a card crowns a leader even when the winning figure is 0%. A **Best conversion** card reading 0% means the team's videos were viewed but nobody clicked a call-to-action, not that the card is broken. When the whole period has no activity, the widget shows a single empty state instead of four blank cards.

![The Top performers widget with a card for each measure: Most views, Best conversion, Most active, and Most engaging](/images/team-insights/top-performers.png)

## Member leaderboard

The leaderboard lists your team members with four columns:

- **Videos**: videos the member created in the period
- **Views**: how many times their video pages were opened
- **CTAs**: call-to-action clicks their videos earned
- **Likes**: thumbs-up likes currently in place on their videos

Click any column heading to rank by it. Ranking is always highest-first, the top three get 🥇🥈🥉, and your own row is tagged **you**. Under each name you'll see their job title, or **Workspace owner** for the owner.

Teams of ten or fewer are shown in full, including members sitting at zero. In a larger workspace the board shows the top ten members who have a number in the column you sorted by, and the footnote tells you how many of your members are on display, reading something like **Showing 4 of 53 members**. Sort by a different column to surface whoever leads it.

<Note>
  The footnote **"Views count per video: one visit on two videos counts twice"** explains why the leaderboard's views can add up to more than the workspace total elsewhere on the dashboard. The leaderboard counts views per video, so one person who opens two of your videos is counted on both.
</Note>

![The Member leaderboard sorted by Views, with medals on the top three members and a you chip marking your own row](/images/team-insights/member-leaderboard.png)

## How each metric is counted

Team Insights is specific about what counts:

- **Attribution follows the campaign creator.** A member's figures cover the campaigns they created, and never change hands afterwards.
- **The period selects videos, not events.** A member's figures cover the videos they created inside the window you chose. Views that land later on an older video don't pull that video back into the report, which is why a longer period can show more views without anyone having watched anything new.
- **Videos** counts what the member created, whether or not anyone watched. A member whose videos went unwatched still shows the videos they made.
- **Views** counts video page opens, the same way the per-video [Analytics modal](/how-to-understand-video-analytics) does. Opening a page is not the same as playing the video.
- **Likes** counts likes that are still in place. If a viewer likes a video and then removes the like, it doesn't count, and liking twice doesn't count twice.
- **CTA rate** counts people, not clicks: the share of unique viewers who clicked a call-to-action. One viewer clicking twice is one conversion.
- **Average watch** is weighted across every video and visit in the period, so in a busy workspace one fully-watched video can't outweigh hundreds that were watched most of the way through. Small samples still swing hard: a member with a single view that ran to the end shows 100% average watch.
- **Rates are left blank, never zero,** when there's nothing to divide by. A member with no views yet shows an empty CTA rate rather than 0%.

<Note>
  Team Insights isn't real time. Figures pass through a few layers of caching, so give a new video or a fresh view two to three minutes to appear. If a member is missing from the leaderboard altogether, that usually means they have nothing in the period you've selected rather than that their numbers are lost.
</Note>

## Customize your dashboard

- **Add widget** opens a drawer with everything available, grouped by category and led by the set recommended for your workspace. The drawer stays open so you can add several in a row, and it has a search box. Press `/` to jump into it. Your dashboard starts with KPI cards, Activity over time, Top performers, Member leaderboard, and Top videos.
- **Drag** a widget by the handle in its header to move it, and drag its bottom-right corner to resize. The grid reflows around it.
- **Remove** a widget with the **✕** in its top-right corner. Adding it back later restores it with its default size.
- **Reset layout** puts the recommended set back.

Layout changes save on their own. The toolbar confirms with **All changes saved**.

Which widgets you can add depends on your plan. On **Solo** the dashboard shows the recommended set, and the rest of the widgets appear in the drawer marked as premium with an upgrade prompt, so you can see what's included before you move up. **Growth**, **Team**, and **Business** include every widget.

![The Add widget drawer open beside the dashboard, showing the recommended widgets for the workspace and the remaining ones grouped by category](/images/team-insights/add-widget-drawer.png)

## Which widgets are available

These twelve widgets are reporting live data today:

| Widget | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **KPI cards** | Total views, engagement rate, conversion rate, watch completion, and CTA clicks for the period |
| **Activity over time** | Plays and engagement plotted across the timeframe |
| **Trend summary** | How the period compares with the one before it |
| **Top performers** | The #1 member for each of four measures |
| **Member leaderboard** | Every member's videos, views, CTAs, and likes |
| **Top videos** | Your best-performing videos, with detail |
| **Content insights** | How your content is performing overall |
| **Conversion rates** | Conversion ratios for the period |
| **Geographic distribution** | Where your viewers are located |
| **Device breakdown** | Viewers by device type |
| **Browser breakdown** | Viewers by browser |
| **OS breakdown** | Viewers by operating system |

Because Team Insights is in beta, the drawer also lists widgets we're still building. You can add them to your layout, but they'll show a **coming soon** placeholder instead of data: Watch funnel, Conversion funnel, Engagement depth, Source breakdown, Top referrers & UTM, Engagement score distribution, Multi-stage funnel, AI insights, and Hot leads.

## Save dashboards as reports

A report is a named dashboard layout, so you can keep one view for your weekly team review and another for a single campaign.

Open the **Report** dropdown in the header to switch between your reports, or to:

- **New report**: save the current layout under a name
- **Duplicate**: copy the report you're on as a starting point
- **Rename**: rename the current report

On any report other than Default you also get **Set as default**, to make it the one that opens first, and **Delete**. The **Default** report itself can be reset but never deleted, which is why those two actions don't appear while you're on it.

Reports save as you work, so treat them as shared rather than private. If another member opens the same report and rearranges it, their layout can replace yours. Private, per-member reports are on the roadmap. Until then, if you want an arrangement nobody else will disturb, save it under a name your team knows to leave alone.

![The Report dropdown open on the Default report, offering New report, Duplicate, and Rename](/images/team-insights/reports-menu.png)

## Export to PDF

Click **Export PDF** to save the dashboard as it appears. The widgets, the reporting period, and any filters you applied are all named in the export, so the file still makes sense weeks later. Wait until the widgets have finished loading before exporting, so nothing lands in the file half-drawn.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does a widget say “coming soon”?">
    Team Insights is in beta and the **Add widget** drawer lists everything planned for the dashboard, including widgets we haven't finished. Adding one keeps its place in your layout and it will start reporting as soon as it ships, so there's no need to add it again. See [which widgets are available](#which-widgets-are-available) for the current list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did Team Overview go?">
    While Team Insights is in beta it takes the place of Team Overview in the sidebar. Your team's numbers are all on this dashboard, and if one you relied on isn't showing, add its widget from **Add widget**. Team Overview's all-time totals are the one exception: Team Insights reports on a period reaching back at most 180 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plans include Team Insights?">
    All of them. On **Solo** the dashboard shows the recommended widget set, and the remaining widgets appear in the **Add widget** drawer marked as premium with an upgrade prompt. **Growth**, **Team**, and **Business** include every widget.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why hasn't my latest video or view appeared yet?">
    Team Insights isn't real time. Figures pass through a few layers of caching, so allow two to three minutes. If a member is missing from the leaderboard entirely, check the reporting period first: they may have created nothing inside the window you've selected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a member show videos but no views?">
    Videos counts what they created; views counts what recipients opened. A member who created videos that nobody has opened yet will show videos with zero views. It's the most common pattern for videos sent in the last day or two.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a member's CTA rate blank instead of 0%?">
    A rate needs viewers to divide by. Until someone has viewed that member's videos there's no denominator, so we leave the rate blank rather than credit them with a 0% they never earned. The same applies to average watch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Best conversion crown someone at 0%?">
    Because there were views to divide by, but no call-to-action clicks. A blank card means nobody has been viewed yet; a card reading 0% means the videos were watched and nobody clicked. Both are working as intended.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do removed likes still count?">
    No. The Likes column counts likes that are currently in place. If a viewer likes a video and then unlikes it, the like disappears from the count, and liking, unliking, then liking again counts once rather than three times.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the leaderboard's views add up to more than the workspace total?">
    The leaderboard counts views per video. One person who opens two of your team's videos in the same visit is counted once on each video, so per-member views can sum to more than the workspace's count of distinct visits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the header say my range was shortened?">
    You asked for a window that reaches further back than the data we hold. The dashboard shows the range it could actually cover and says what you originally asked for, so you're never comparing a 90-day figure against a shorter one by accident.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My team has more than ten people. Where is everyone?">
    The board shows the top ten for whichever column you sorted by, and the footnote says how many of your members are on display. Sort by a different column to see who leads that one. Everyone is included in the ranking; only the display is trimmed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does filtering by member change the leaderboard?">
    Yes. Every widget on the dashboard, including the leaderboard and Top performers, follows the filters and the reporting period you've set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are my saved reports private to me?">
    No, treat them as shared. If another member opens the same report and rearranges it, their layout can replace yours. Private, per-member reports are on the roadmap. Until then, keep a layout you rely on under a name your team knows to leave alone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from a single video's analytics?">
    The per-video [Analytics modal](/how-to-understand-video-analytics) answers "who watched this video". Team Insights answers "how is the team doing" across every campaign in the period, broken down by the member who created it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
