title: "Embed Google Calendar with Videos"
description: "Learn how to embed a Google Calendar booking link with your Sendspark videos. Let prospects schedule meetings directly from your video landing page."
---

To embed a Google Calendar with your Sendspark video, first create an **Appointment schedule** in Google Calendar and copy its booking page URL. Then, under your Sendspark video, toggle Calendar to On, select **Google** from the provider dropdown, and paste the link. This only works for Google Workspace or Business accounts.

## Key takeaways

- This procedure only works for Google Workspace or Business accounts.
- In Google Calendar, click +Create, choose **Appointment schedule**, then open the booking page and copy its URL.
- In Sendspark, toggle Calendar to On, select **Google** as the provider, and paste in the booking link.
- Your embedded Google Calendar appears in the preview area, letting prospects book meetings right after watching your video.

### Embed your Google calendar next to your Sendspark videos to help prospects book meetings right after watching.

This procedure only works for Google Workspaces or Business accounts

## Steps to follow In Google:

1. Head to your calendar app: [https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r)

![The Google apps launcher grid, with Calendar among the apps](/images/9211984/7b97cf43-image.png)

2. On the left-hand side corner, click on +Create and then on Appointment Schedule

![Google Calendar left sidebar with Create button and Appointment Schedule option](/images/9211984/960fc176-image.png)

3. Set your title and the **Scheduling window** and click on Next:

![Google Calendar appointment schedule creation form with title and time fields](/images/9211984/1c74ebab-image.png)

4. Finish your desired settings and click **Save**:

![The Save button that commits the appointment schedule](/images/9211984/6b6779c6-image.png)

5. A pop-up window will come up. Click **Preview** to open the booking page:

![The appointment schedule pop-up, offering a preview of the booking page and a control to copy its link](/images/9211984/de18dbb8-image.png)

6. You should now be on a new page. Kindly copy its URL:

![Google Calendar booking page URL in address bar ready to copy](/images/9211984/9907767f-image.png)

## Steps to follow In Sendspark:

1. Under your Sendspark video, toggle **"Calendar"** to **"On"**

2. Select **"Google Calendar"** from the Provider Dropdown List

**3. Add the link under **Link (URL)****to the Google calendar you want to use

![Sendspark video editor showing Calendar toggle set to On and Google Calendar provider selected](/images/9211984/4fa7c2f3-image.png)

You should be able to see your embedded Google calendar in the preview area!
