A dynamic video campaign can use the same thumbnail settings as a normal video, including a banner with variables such as `{{firstname}}`. In the **Landing Page** step of your campaign, click **Edit** on the **Email Thumbnail** row, write your banner, and save. Each contact then gets a thumbnail with their own name on it, for example "Hey Bethany!", on both the image and the GIF.

## Key takeaways

- The **Email Thumbnail** row is in the **Landing Page** step of every dynamic video campaign. Click **Edit** to open the thumbnail editor.
- The editor is the same one used for normal videos: banner text with variables, the play button, the logo and its shape, the preview frame and the duration style.
- New contacts get the banner automatically. For contacts whose videos are already generated, click **Apply to N videos** to update their thumbnails.
- The preview panel has an **Email Thumbnail** tab where you can see the real image or GIF for any contact with **Preview as**.
- A campaign without thumbnail settings does not change.

## How to add a banner to your campaign thumbnails

1. Open your campaign from [Dynamic Videos](https://sendspark.com/dynamic-videos) and go to the **Landing Page** step.
2. Scroll to the **Email Thumbnail** row and click **Edit**.
3. Under **Text Overlay**, type your banner text. Click **Personalize** to add a variable such as the contact's first name.
4. Optionally add a logo, and adjust the play button and other thumbnail styles. See [How to edit video thumbnails](/how-to-edit-video-thumbnails) for what each setting does.
5. Click **Save**.

## Preview the thumbnail for a contact

In the preview panel on the right, open the **Email Thumbnail** tab. Choose a contact under **Preview as**, then switch between **Image** and **GIF** to see exactly what that contact receives. The image and the GIF use the same settings.

## Update videos you already generated

Your edits apply to new videos only. If the campaign already has generated videos, a notice at the top of the Landing Page step tells you how many still use the previous landing page or thumbnail. Click **Apply to N videos** to update them.

This also means you can fix a mistake at any time: come back to this step, edit the banner, and apply the change again.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find the Email Thumbnail settings?">
    Open your dynamic video campaign, go to the Landing Page step, and click Edit on the Email Thumbnail row.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the banner include each contact's name?">
    Yes. Click Personalize in the thumbnail editor to add a variable such as the first name. Each contact gets the banner with their own name, on the image and on the GIF.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I already generated my videos. Can I still add or change the banner?">
    Yes. Edit the thumbnail, then click Apply to N videos at the top of the Landing Page step to update the thumbnails of the contacts that are already generated.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does this change my existing campaigns?">
    No. A campaign without thumbnail settings does not change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
