## Sending dynamic videos with Nooks

The Sendspark + Nooks integration lets you send dynamic videos in your Nooks email sequences. Instead of recording a separate video for each prospect, create one video and Sendspark automatically personalizes it for every recipient — greeting them by name, showing their website, and more.

Nooks is an AI-powered sales engagement platform that combines email sequencing, parallel dialing, buying signals, and AI coaching into one workspace for outbound sales teams.

## How to send dynamic videos in Nooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish your dynamic video campaign">
    In Sendspark, create and generate your Dynamic Video campaign. If you're new to dynamic videos, check out our [getting started guide](/create-a-dynamic-video-campaign) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Nooks snippet">
    In Sendspark, click **Share Campaign** and select **Nooks** from the list of compatible platforms. This copies the share snippet to your clipboard. The snippet contains an animated thumbnail and a "Watch video" link with the `{{email}}` merge tag.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of Sendspark Share Campaign dialog with Nooks selected */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the snippet into a Nooks email step">
    In Nooks, create or edit an email step in your sequence. Paste the Sendspark snippet directly into the email body.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of Nooks email editor with snippet pasted */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your email">
    In the Nooks editor, you'll see the default version of your video (a video of yourself). This is expected — the video personalizes automatically once the email is sent to each contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to the right contacts">
    Make sure the contacts in your Nooks sequence match the prospects you generated videos for in Sendspark. When Nooks sends the email, each recipient's `{{email}}` is replaced with their actual email address. Sendspark uses this to match the contact to their personalized video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To be safe, click **Download CSV** in Sendspark to get a clean list of contacts with successfully generated videos. Upload that list to Nooks so you don't email anyone who didn't get a video.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What format does the Nooks snippet use?">
    The Nooks snippet uses the same format as Apollo: an animated thumbnail GIF linked to the personalized video page, plus a "Watch video" text link. Both links contain the `{{email}}` variable so Sendspark can match each recipient to their video.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the animated thumbnail doesn't display properly?">
    If Nooks strips the thumbnail from the snippet, contact Sendspark support. We can switch the snippet to a plain-link format that works in any email client.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track video views when sending through Nooks?">
    Yes. All video views, play events, and engagement data appear in your Sendspark dashboard regardless of which platform you use to send the email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why should I download Sendspark's CSV instead of using my own list?">
    When you generate videos for many contacts, some may not render — for example, a prospect's website may fail to load. Sendspark's exported CSV only includes contacts whose video was successfully generated, so you don't email anyone who would see a broken placeholder.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
