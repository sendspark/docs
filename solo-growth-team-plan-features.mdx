Sendspark offers four plans. **Solo ($49/mo)** includes 1 seat and 100 dynamic video minutes, **Growth ($99/mo)** includes 3 seats and 250 minutes, **Team ($299/mo)** includes 10 seats and 1,000 minutes, and **Business ($699/mo)** includes 25 seats and 3,000 minutes. AI personalization, dynamic backgrounds, and your own brand styles are on every plan. A custom domain, branding removal, advanced integrations, and API access start at Growth.

## Key takeaways

- **Solo ($49/mo)**: 1 seat, 100 dynamic video minutes, AI personalization, dynamic backgrounds, brand styles, and basic sending integrations (Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn, HubSpot).
- **Growth ($99/mo)**: 3 seats, 250 minutes, plus advanced integrations, CRM tracking, API and webhooks, multiple AI variables, agentic workflows, an admin dashboard, a custom domain, and branding removal.
- **Team ($299/mo)**: 10 seats, 1,000 minutes, everything in Growth plus guided workflow setup.
- **Business ($699/mo)**: 25 seats, 3,000 minutes, everything in Team.
- **A custom domain and branding removal start at Growth**, not Solo. So do API and webhooks, advanced integrations, and CRM tracking. Your own logo and colors do work on Solo.
- Extra seats can be added to any plan, and the per-seat price falls as the tier rises.

## Solo Plan

💲 **$49 /mo** (1 seat included, extra seats +$49/mo)
👤 Best for individuals who want to create AI-personalized videos.

### What's included

- **100 Dynamic Video Minutes** per month
- **AI Name Personalization** (automatically personalizes videos with viewer names)
- **Dynamic Video Backgrounds** (add branded or customized backdrops)
- **Basic sending integrations**: Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn, HubSpot
- **Brand styles**: your own logo and colors on the video page, via [Global Styles](/set-global-styles)
- Unlimited regular (non-dynamic) videos

### Not included on Solo

- Custom domain (CNAME), so videos stay on a Sendspark URL
- Removing the Sendspark badge
- API and webhooks
- Multiple AI variables and agentic workflows
- Advanced sending integrations such as Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo and Smartlead
- CRM tracking integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho)

## Growth Plan

💲 **$99 /mo** (includes 3 seats, extra seats +$45/mo)
👥 Best for small teams that want to scale video selling and integrations.

### Everything in Solo, plus:

- **250 Dynamic Video Minutes** per month
- **3 Seats Included** in the price, expandable for larger teams
- **Advanced integrations**, 50+ email, CRM and sales engagement platforms including Salesforce, Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo, Smartlead, Zoho, Pipedrive, Mailchimp and ActiveCampaign
- **Native Integrations**: Clay, Expandi, Zapier
- **API & Webhooks Access** (automate workflows, advanced customizations)
- **Multiple AI Variables** and **Agentic Workflows**
- **Custom Domain (CNAME)** (host videos under your own domain) and **branding removal** (drop the Sendspark badge)
- **CRM tracking integrations** ([Salesforce](/how-to-set-up-sendspark-for-salesforce), [HubSpot](/how-to-set-up-sendspark-for-hubspot-crm-integration), [Zoho](/how-to-set-up-sendspark-for-zoho)), which push video-page visits into your CRM
- **Admin Dashboard** (manage teams, permissions, and usage centrally)

## ✅ Which Plan Should I Choose?

- **Choose Solo** if you're an individual creator or salesperson who needs 1 seat and moderate video volume, and you don't need your own domain or API access.
- **Choose Growth** if you need advanced integrations, CRM tracking, API access, or a white-label experience on your own domain. This is the first tier with a custom domain and branding removal.
- **Choose Team** if you need 10 or more seats, roughly 1,000 minutes a month, or help getting your automations set up.
- **Choose Business** if you need 25 seats and 3,000 minutes, at the lowest per-seat price.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many seats does each plan include?">
    Solo includes 1 seat, Growth includes 3, Team includes 10, and Business includes 25. Extra seats can be added to any plan, and the per-seat price drops as the tier rises: +$49/mo on Solo, +$45 on Growth, +$39 on Team, and +$29 on Business.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many dynamic video minutes come with each plan?">
    Solo includes 100 dynamic video minutes per month, Growth 250, Team 1,000, and Business 3,000. See [what are dynamic video minutes / credits](/what-are-dynamic-video-minutes-credits) for how they're counted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plans include a custom domain?">
    A custom domain (CNAME) starts on **Growth**. It is not included on Solo. If you're on Solo and want your videos served from your own domain, you'll need to upgrade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I put my own logo on videos with the Solo plan?">
    Yes. Brand styles, your logo and colors, work on every plan including Solo, and you set them once in [Global Styles](/set-global-styles). What Solo does not include is a custom domain or removing the Sendspark badge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove Sendspark branding on the Solo plan?">
    No. Removing the Sendspark badge starts on Growth. You can still apply your own logo and colors on Solo.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plans include API and webhooks access?">
    API and webhooks are included on Growth, Team and Business. They are not available on Solo.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do all plans include AI personalization?">
    Yes. AI personalization and dynamic backgrounds are on every plan, including Solo. Multiple AI variables and agentic workflows start on Growth.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many integrations are supported?">
    Growth and above connect with 50+ sales, CRM and marketing platforms, including Salesforce, Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo, Smartlead, Zoho, Pipedrive and Mailchimp. Solo includes basic sending integrations only: Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn and HubSpot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a sending integration and CRM tracking?">
    A sending integration puts your video into an email or sequence in another tool. CRM tracking sends video-page visits back into your CRM so engagement shows on the contact record. They're gated separately: Solo includes basic sending integrations, while CRM tracking starts on Growth.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
    No. Sendspark has a 7-day free trial of the full product rather than a free tier. See [how to create a free trial](/how-to-create-a-free-trial).
