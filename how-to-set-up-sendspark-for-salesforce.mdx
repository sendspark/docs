To connect Sendspark with Salesforce, open your [Sendspark integrations tab](https://sendspark.com/settings/integrations), click **Connect** next to Salesforce, enter your Salesforce **Business Unit Account ID**, and save. This sends Sendspark video-page visits to Salesforce so you can track engagement across leads, contacts, and user records and visualize it in reports and dashboards. Salesforce is an advanced integration, so it needs the Growth plan or above.

## Key takeaways

- The integration sends Sendspark video-page visits to Salesforce so you can track engagement with leads, contacts, and user records and visualize it in reports and dashboards.
- It's available on **every Sendspark plan**; you also need an active Salesforce Experience account with access to your business unit ID.
- You connect the tools from the [Sendspark integrations tab](https://sendspark.com/settings/integrations) by clicking **Connect** next to Salesforce and entering your Salesforce Business Unit Account ID. For the wider setup, including sending video emails straight from your CRM, see [connect Sendspark with Salesforce](/connect-sendspark-with-salesforce).
- Once saved, Salesforce shows an **Enabled** status and its button changes to **Edit**. The video below shows where to find your Salesforce Business Unit Account ID.

The Sendspark for Salesforce integration allows you to measure the impact video is having on your business.

This integration will allow you to send Sendspark page visits to Salesforce to track engagement with individual leads, contacts, and user records, and visualize video engagement data in reports and dashboards.

## Requirements

- An active Sendspark account on the **Growth plan or above**, since Salesforce is an advanced integration (see [plan features](/solo-growth-team-plan-features))
- An active Salesforce Experience account with access to your business unit ID

## Integrate Sendspark with Salesforce

1. Go to your [Sendspark integrations tab](https://sendspark.com/settings/integrations)
2. Next to Salesforce, click **Connect** ​![Sendspark integrations tab with Connect button next to Salesforce](/images/5970577/bebac7f5-image.png)
3. Enter your Salesforce**Business Unit Account ID:**![The Salesforce row in the integrations list, with a field for the business unit account ID](/images/5970577/141a1237-image.png)
4. **Save** changes
5. You should see that Salesforce is **enabled** now 🎉 ​![Sendspark integrations tab showing The Salesforce integration showing an Enabled status, with its button now reading Edit](/images/5970577/4279024e-image.png)
