How you capture your computer's system audio depends on where you record. The **desktop app records it automatically**, with nothing to switch on. In the **Chrome Extension**, system audio is off by default, so to catch internal sounds like a YouTube video, webinar, or music you pick **Record Tab** and enable **Share Tab Audio**.

## Key takeaways

- The **desktop app records system audio automatically**. There's no toggle to find.
- In the **Chrome Extension**, use the **Record Tab** option and tick **Share Tab Audio**.
- By default the Chrome Extension captures microphone audio (and speaker audio your mic picks up from the room), but not the system audio stream.
- The Record Tab route works only in Google Chrome. You can enable microphone plus tab audio together to capture your voice and the system sound at once.
- For the per-platform detail, including what differs between Mac and Windows, see [record screen and computer audio](/record-screen-and-computer-audio).

## Which method to use

<Note>
  Recording in the desktop app? Stop here, it already captures system audio. The steps below are only needed in the Chrome Extension, where system audio is off by default.
</Note>

In the Chrome Extension, recordings capture:

- **Microphone audio** (your voice).
- **Speaker audio** (if your mic picks it up from the room).

That does **not** include the actual **system audio stream** from your computer, so you need to ask for it explicitly using **Record Tab** and **Share Tab Audio**.

## How to record system audio in the Chrome Extension

1. [Click here to install the Chrome Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/sendspark-video-and-scree/blimjkpadkhcpmkeboeknjcmiaogbkph)
2. Open the Chrome tab that has the audio you want to record (e.g. YouTube, webinar, music).
3. Click the **Sendspark Chrome Extension** icon.
4. Choose **Record Tab** as your source.
5. When prompted, check the box for **Share Tab Audio**.
6. Start recording. Sendspark captures both the video of the tab and its system audio.

![Sendspark Chrome Extension Record Tab dialog with Share Tab Audio checkbox option](/images/12268069/28c4097a-image.png)

## Notes and limitations

- The Record Tab route works only in **Google Chrome**.
- The **desktop app** needs none of this, because it records system audio automatically.
- You can also enable both **microphone + tab audio** if you want to record your voice *and* the system sound together.

## Frequently asked questions

  <Accordion title="Can the desktop app record system audio?">
    Yes, and it does so automatically, so there's nothing to enable. The Record Tab and Share Tab Audio steps on this page apply to the Chrome Extension, where system audio is off by default. See [record screen and computer audio](/record-screen-and-computer-audio).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I capture the sound from a YouTube video or webinar?">
    In the Chrome Extension, open the tab with the audio, click the Sendspark Chrome Extension, choose Record Tab as your source, and check "Share Tab Audio" when prompted. Sendspark then captures both the tab's video and its system audio. In the desktop app, just record as usual.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I record my voice and the system audio together?">
    Yes. You can enable both microphone and tab audio so your voice and the system sound are recorded at the same time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which browser is required?">
    The Record Tab method needs Google Chrome with the Sendspark Chrome Extension. If you'd rather not be tied to Chrome, the [desktop app](/how-to-use-the-sendspark-desktop-application) records system audio automatically.
  </Accordion>
