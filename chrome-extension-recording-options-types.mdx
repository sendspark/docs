The Sendspark Chrome Extension gives you three recording options: **Camera Only**, **Screen Only**, or **Cam + Screen** (your camera bubble over your screen). When you record Screen Only or Cam + Screen, you then pick what to share: Chrome Tab, Window, or Entire Screen. Camera Only has nothing to pick, because it captures only your camera.

## Key takeaways

- There are three recording options: **Camera Only**, **Screen Only**, and **Cam + Screen** (camera over screen).
- With Screen Only or Cam + Screen selected, you also choose what to share: **Chrome Tab**, **Window**, or **Entire Screen**.
- Camera Only has nothing to share, because only your camera is captured.
- The face camera works seamlessly on Chrome, but some apps in full-screen mode can override Sendspark's bubble recording.

### Recording options

| Option | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| Camera Only | Your camera only |
| Screen (Screen Only) | Your screen only |
| Cam + Screen | Your camera over your screen |

### Recording types

| Type | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| Chrome Tab | Only what's below the address bar |
| Window | The whole window of the application you selected |
| Entire Screen | Your entire screen/desktop, including folders and anything on screen |

## Recording Options

There are three options to record with the **Chrome Extension**.

![The Sendspark recorder toolbar with its recording mode icons](/images/5152208/1e8f036a-new.png)

**Camera Only**

With this option, you'll be able to record only your camera.

**Screen (Screen Only)**

With this option selected, you'll be able to record only your screen.

**Cam + Screen**

With this option selected, you'll be able to record your camera over your screen.

**⚠ Please note:**The face camera feature in Sendspark is designed to work seamlessly on Chrome. However, there are instances where certain apps with a full-screen mode can override Sendspark's bubble recording functionality.

With each recording option, you can choose a recording type as well, to get your recordings just right!

## Recording Types

Once you've clicked **Camera Only**, **Screen Only**, or **Cam + Screen** you are almost ready to start. Click **Record** and a new modal will pop up:

![Chrome's share window with the Chrome Tab, Window and Entire Screen panes](/images/5152208/a8774b22-image.png)

**⚠ Please note:** You can't change any of the sharing options for **Camera Only**, since we're only recording your camera.

**Chrome Tab**

This option will only record what's below the address bar.

![Diagram of a browser window with a single tab's content highlighted](/images/5152208/c0d0ed8e-recording-types-breakdown-current-tab.png)

**Application window**

This option will record the whole application window of the application you've selected

![Diagram of a desktop with one application window highlighted](/images/5152208/ad114dc2-recording-types-breakdown-application-window.png)

**Entire screen**

This option will record your entire screen/desktop - folders, anything on the screen.

![Diagram of a whole desktop screen highlighted](/images/5152208/118f4ceb-recording-types-breakdown-entire-screen.png)

👋 Happy recording!
