To password protect a Sendspark video, open the video, click the lock icon in the top bar, type a password or click **Generate**, and click **Save Password**. From then on, viewers have to enter the password before they can watch.

## Key takeaways

- The password is set per video, from that video's edit page.
- Click the **lock icon** in the top bar. Its tooltip reads **Set Password**.
- Type your own password, or click **Generate** to have Sendspark create one for you.
- Click **Save Password** to turn the protection on. Viewers must enter the password before the video plays.

## How to set a password on a video

1. Go to [My Library](https://sendspark.com/my-library) and click the video you want to protect. This opens its edit page.
2. Click the **lock icon** in the top bar.
3. Type a password, or click **Generate** to create one automatically.
4. Click **Save Password**.
5. Share the video link as usual, and send the password to the people who should be able to watch.
