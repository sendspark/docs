Want to send personalized Sendspark videos to your GoHighLevel contacts via SMS without setting up automations? You can do it manually using a CSV file and a Dynamic Video Campaign.

This method is ideal for teams that want to run personalized SMS outreach without configuring Agentic Workflows.

**Plan requirements:** Bulk video generation may require the Growth plan or higher, depending on your current plan and usage. If you're on the Solo plan, you can still use this manual approach if your account has access to the required features.

## How It Works

1. Prepare your contact list in a CSV file.
2. Use your list to generate personalized videos in Sendspark.
3. Retrieve the generated video links.
4. Use those links in your GoHighLevel SMS outreach.
